2. General information

This Privacy Policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit this website or contact us voluntarily. Personal data is information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

The website is technically implemented as a static HTML, CSS and JavaScript website. Processing when a page is requested is carried out primarily by the intended hosting provider. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

3. Hosting by IONOS

For the final operation of the website, classic Linux web hosting is intended to be provided by:

IONOS SE
Elgendorfer Straße 57
56410 Montabaur
Germany

When the website is accessed, the hosting provider processes technically necessary access and log data. According to the current IONOS information on web hosting, this includes:

  • the referrer or previously visited page,
  • the requested website or file,
  • browser type and browser version,
  • the operating system and device type used,
  • the time of access, and
  • the IP address in anonymised form.

This processing serves the secure provision of the website, the stability of the service, error analysis, protection against misuse and technical optimisation. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in the secure, stable and technically reliable operation of the website.

According to the hosting provider, visitor data is stored for eight weeks, is not disclosed to third parties and is not transferred to countries outside the European Union.

Processing on behalf of the controller is governed by an agreement pursuant to Article 28 GDPR. According to IONOS, this agreement has formed part of the IONOS terms and conditions for new contracts since 19 July 2022. The actual contractual status will be checked in the IONOS account before the website is launched.

4. IONOS WebAnalytics

According to IONOS, IONOS WebAnalytics is enabled by default for the intended web hosting. Analysis is carried out using log data or a pixel and does not use cookies. The IP address transmitted when a page is requested is anonymised immediately after transmission and subsequently processed without any personal reference. This is intended to prevent the direct identification of individual visitors.

The information processed comprises the referrer, requested page or file, browser type and version, operating system, device type, access time and the anonymised IP address or the access location derived from it.

Processing is carried out for statistical analysis, technical optimisation of the website and the detection of technical problems. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in technical improvement, stability and anonymised reach and usage analysis. According to IONOS, no data is disclosed to third parties.

5. Cookies and local storage

The website code does not set cookies and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage. No advertising, marketing or profiling cookies are used. According to IONOS, IONOS WebAnalytics also operates without cookies.

Based on the current technical configuration, no cookie consent banner is used. Should functions requiring consent be added in future, this Privacy Policy and the technical consent solution will be updated accordingly.

6. Contact by email or telephone

If you contact us voluntarily by email or telephone, we process the data you provide. This may include your name, contact details, the content of your enquiry and, where applicable, information about an order or event.

Processing is carried out to handle and answer your enquiry, communicate with you and prepare or perform a contractual relationship. If your enquiry concerns a contract or pre-contractual measures, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Otherwise, processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries.

The data will be deleted once the enquiry has been fully dealt with, provided that no statutory retention obligations or legitimate grounds require further storage.

7. Links to Instagram and Facebook

The website contains only ordinary links to Instagram and Facebook. Merely visiting this website does not establish a connection to Instagram or Facebook.

Only when you actively select one of these links will the website of the relevant platform open. From that point onwards, the platform may process personal data in accordance with its own privacy terms. Further information is available in Meta’s Privacy Policy.

8. SSL/TLS encryption

The final website is intended to be delivered exclusively over HTTPS. The encrypted connection protects data in transit between your browser and the web server. Encryption cannot, however, eliminate every security risk.

9. Rights of data subjects

Subject to the statutory requirements, you have the following rights in particular:

  • the right of access to personal data being processed,
  • the right to rectification of inaccurate data,
  • the right to erasure,
  • the right to restriction of processing,
  • the right to data portability, where applicable,
  • the right to object, and
  • the right to withdraw consent with effect for the future.

You may use the contact details above to exercise your rights.

10. Right to object

Under Article 21 GDPR, you have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of personal data based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Following an objection, we will no longer process the relevant data unless there are compelling legitimate grounds for processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is required for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

11. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The regional supervisory authority for North Rhine-Westphalia is:

State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia
www.ldi.nrw.de

12. Effective date and changes

This Privacy Policy reflects the currently intended technical configuration of the website and its hosting. Changes to the website, hosting or legal requirements may require this policy to be updated.
